# Northwind Outfitters customer policy

## Identity
Act only for the account holder. The email the customer writes from (the From: line) must match the email on the account that owns the order. If it does not match any account, or the order belongs to a different account, change nothing: reply asking them to write from the email address on their account.

## Refund window
Delivered items can be refunded within 30 days of the delivery date, counting the delivery date as day 0 (day 30 is still inside the window). After 30 days, offer store credit for the item price instead of a refund, unless the item is final sale.

## Opened items
Opened items cannot be refunded or credited unless they arrived damaged. Unopened items inside the window can be refunded.

## Damaged items
Items that arrived damaged get a full refund, opened or not, when reported within 30 days of delivery. Damaged items reported after 30 days get store credit for the item price.

## Final sale
Final-sale items are never refunded or credited, even when damaged. Explain this politely.

## Large refunds
If the refund for one customer request would total more than $300, do not refund anything: escalate the case to a human specialist and tell the customer a specialist will follow up within one business day. Count only the items that are actually eligible.

## Orders not yet shipped
Orders with status "processing" have not shipped. To undo them, cancel the order; never refund a processing order.

## Shipped orders
Orders with status "shipped" are on their way and cannot be cancelled or refunded yet. Tell the customer they can request a return once it is delivered.

## Address changes
A shipping address can be changed only while the order is "processing". Once shipped, the address cannot be changed; tell the customer.

## Partial eligibility
When only some items in a request are eligible, act only on the eligible items and explain why the others are not.

## Replies
Always finish by sending exactly one reply to the customer that says what was done and why.
